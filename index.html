Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how List It ("the App"), developed by LK Games, handles information when you use the application.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

02 Information Stored on Your Device

List It stores information locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

This may include:

  • Task lists and their names.
  • Task titles and descriptions.
  • Task deadlines and completion status.
  • Tags and their colors.
  • App preferences, themes and settings.
  • Reminder preferences and related notification information.

You may enter personal or sensitive information into task titles and descriptions. Please consider this when deciding what to store.

03 Data Collection and Sharing

The App does not currently provide user accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics or in-app tracking.

The App does not operate a service that collects or transmits your task information to the developer.

We do not sell or share your task data with third parties.

04 Local Notifications

List It can schedule local notifications to remind you about upcoming task deadlines.

Notification content may include task titles, list names and reminder timing.

Notifications are generated locally on your device. Depending on your device settings, notification content may be visible on your lock screen.

You can manage notification permissions and reminder preferences through your device and the App.

05 Data Backups

List It disables Android Auto Backup and excludes its local task database from the configured Android cloud backup and device-transfer rules.

Your task data is therefore not intended to be included in Android backups through these mechanisms.

This means your data may not automatically transfer to a new device or be restored after reinstalling the App.

06 Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored locally until you delete it or remove the App's data.

The App provides a reset function that clears locally stored application data and restores the default application state.

Resetting the App does not necessarily remove notifications that have already been delivered to your device.

07 Permissions

The App may request Android notification-related permissions to support scheduled deadline reminders.

Permissions can be managed through your Android device settings.

08 Children's Privacy

List It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account or data-collection service.

09 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy address.
